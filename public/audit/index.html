01 — THE PROBLEM

Every portfolio older than one board has conflicts hiding in it.

A 2002 declaration sets one late fee. A 2023 resolution sets another. An architectural guideline approves door colors the covenants ban. Minutes cite a rental cap that doesn't match the recorded amendment. It isn't negligence — it's what happens when more than one board governs over time.

Your management platform's AI can't catch this. It only knows what lives inside your platform. Your declarations, amendments, ARC guidelines, and thirty years of minutes don't live there — they live in file servers, shared drives, and the Register of Deeds.

And it costs you twice. Time: managers lose hours a week digging through PDFs for answers. Exposure: every violation letter or permit that cites the wrong document becomes a written record a homeowner's attorney can find later. The audit maps those conflicts before someone else does.

The recorded rental cap was 20% — thirty-six units. The board had been issuing permits against 25%. Every permit above thirty-six was above the legal cap.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the audit require access to our management software?

No. It's documents-only. You export folders; we never request credentials to your management platform, accounting, banking, or email. System connections happen only in a full deployment, inside your own environment.

Where are our documents processed?

On Deepvine's own private stack in the U.S., under a signed data handling agreement. Your documents never touch a public AI tool or shared cloud model, are never used for training, and are deleted with a written certificate within ten business days. On-site processing is available on request.

Is homeowner personal information involved?

No. Owner ledgers, delinquency records, contact lists, and payment information are expressly excluded from scope. Most audit documents are recorded instruments that are already public record.

What if you don't find anything?

Then it's free. Fewer than five documented findings and we refund the fee in full.

How long does the audit take?

Ten business days from documents received to report delivered, including your live working session.

Does the fee count toward a full deployment?

Yes, all of it. 100% of the audit fee is credited against a deployment signed within 90 days of your Findings Report.
