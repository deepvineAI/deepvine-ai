The Association Knowledge Audit

The Association Knowledge Audit is a fixed-fee engagement where Deepvine ingests two to three of your associations' complete governing document sets into a private AI environment, surfaces the conflicts and gaps hiding across declarations, amendments, guidelines, policies, and minutes, and delivers a written Findings Report your boards and counsel can act on. It is documents-only: no system access, no owner data in scope, and nothing ever touches a public AI tool. 01

Why do governing documents need an audit?

Because they contradict each other, and your staff answers around the contradictions every day without knowing it. A declaration recorded in 2002 sets one late fee; a collections resolution adopted in 2023 sets another. An architectural guideline approves door colors the covenants prohibit. Board minutes cite a rental cap that doesn't match the recorded amendment. None of this is negligence — it is the natural state of any association that has been governed by more than one board.

The cost shows up two ways. The first is time: managers spend hours a week digging through PDFs to answer questions the documents could answer in seconds. The second is exposure: every violation letter, permit, and phone answer that cites the wrong document is a written record a homeowner's attorney can find later. The audit maps those conflicts before someone else does.

03

How do you handle our documents?

Carefully, and in writing. The audit runs under a signed data handling agreement before a single file moves. It is documents-only: we never request credentials to your management platform, accounting, banking, or email. Owner ledgers, delinquency records, contact lists, and payment information are expressly excluded from scope — and most of what we do audit consists of recorded instruments that are already public record at the Register of Deeds.

Processing happens on Deepvine's own private stack in the United States — the same architecture we deploy for clients — never on a shared cloud model. When your report is delivered, we permanently delete your documents and every derived index, and issue a written certificate of deletion. Firms that prefer the data never leave their building can request on-site processing at their offices. You can read more about how we build on our security page.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the audit require access to our management software?

No. The audit is documents-only. You export folders; we never request credentials to your management platform, accounting, banking, or email. System connections happen only in a full deployment, inside your own environment.

Where are our documents processed?

On Deepvine's own private stack in the United States, under a signed data handling agreement. Your documents never touch a public AI tool or shared cloud model, are never used for training, and are deleted with a written certificate within ten business days of your report. On-site processing at your offices is available on request.

Is homeowner personal information involved?

No. Owner ledgers, delinquency records, contact lists, and payment information are expressly excluded from audit scope. Most audit documents are recorded instruments that are already public record.

What if you don't find anything?

Then the audit is free. If your Findings Report contains fewer than five documented findings, we refund the fee in full. We offer that guarantee because in our experience, every portfolio older than one board has conflicts waiting to be found.

How long does the audit take?

Ten business days from documents received to report delivered, including your live working session.

Does the fee count toward a full deployment?

Yes, all of it. 100% of the audit fee is credited against a deployment signed within 90 days of your Findings Report.
